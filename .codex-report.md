- Rivals 2's infinite loading and the log-pending Gravity Circuit/Drawful 2 exits do not establish their root causes.
- Microsoft's Xbox UWP resource table documents native D3D11/D3D12 at feature level 11.0 on Series X|S, and separate app/game memory budgets. Native D3D12 availability is not proof that a UE5 or RE Engine workload fits. Primary documentation links are embedded in the plan.
