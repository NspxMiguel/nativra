# What the licence means, in one paragraph

This project is free to use, read, change and share. It may not be sold, and
it may not be used to make money — no paid builds, no paid "support" around it,
no bundling it into something with a price tag. Everything else is open:
personal use, study, hobby projects, translations, forks, contributions.

The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) — the PolyForm Noncommercial License
1.0.0, which exists precisely for this and is better drafted than anything
written from scratch.

## What this project is not

It is not a way to play games you do not own. It talks to Steam with the
player's own account, downloads what that account already owns, and runs it.
There is no license bypass here and none will be accepted.
